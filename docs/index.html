Multiplayer agents that cooperate with humans, not just with themselves

FACET (Fictitious Agent Co-training for Emergent Teamwork) trains reinforcement-learning agents against a prioritized population of human behavior models and frozen self-play checkpoints, so they keep cooperating when their partner isn't a copy of themselves. Everything below is a real trained model replayed in your browser — trained on the Overcooked-AI benchmark with 103,141 timesteps of real human–human gameplay.

How it works

Four stages, all reproducible from the repo on a laptop.

1Human data → BC

Behavior-cloning networks are fit to the 2019 Overcooked human–human dataset (five layouts, train/test split by game), giving stochastic human models with 54–75% held-out action accuracy.

2Self-play PPO

Parameter-shared PPO agents are trained from scratch per layout with annealed reward shaping. They score highly with themselves — and their checkpoints become a skill-diverse partner zoo.

3FACET population

A best-response agent trains against a pool of BC human models + frozen self-play checkpoints. Sampling is prioritized toward the partners it currently coordinates worst with.

4Cross-play eval

Every agent is paired with held-out human proxies it never trained with, in both seats. That's the score that matters for multiplayer workspaces — not self-play reward.
